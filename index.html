02 Intuit Accountant Suite

Challenges → leadership response

Five constraints. No room to move the date.

The opportunity

Replace a legacy accountant experience with a strategic new platform—aligning Product, Engineering, Design, Data, Marketing, Sales, and Care while protecting launch readiness across critical dependencies.

ChallengeAction taken
01Ambiguous, changing requirementsSeparated launch-critical scope from fast follows and made every change visible through the decision log.
02Intuit Connect date is fixedWorked backward from the immovable date, built the critical path, and used readiness gates to protect it.
03Decisions were taking too longNamed the decision owner, set closure dates, and brought unresolved tradeoffs to the right executive forum.
04Design capacity was scarceFocused design resources on launch-critical journeys and surfaced priority tradeoffs before they became schedule risks.
05Monetization team was on the critical pathMade it a named dependency with accountable owners, milestone reviews, and targeted triage until closure.

Weekly decisions

Focused leaders on hotspots, tradeoffs, and decisions—not broad status reporting.

Prioritization & scale

Grouped requests into capability programs, exposed capacity collisions, and sequenced work against the critical path.

Metrics & dashboard

Tracked owner coverage, alignment state, aging decisions, dependency hotspots, commitment confidence, and escalation volume.

Team enablement

Created templates, decision rules, working cadences, and coaching so TPMs and leads could operate the model consistently.

Risk management

Separated unresolved decisions from delivery risks, assigned closure dates, and escalated only where leadership intervention changed the outcome.
