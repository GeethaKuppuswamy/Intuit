02 Intuit Accountant Suite

Execution + challenge management

Protect the core launch. Make the tradeoff explicit. Fast-follow what is valuable.

The pivotal decision

A late request expanded the metrics dashboard and put the launch path at risk.

I reframed the debate from “dashboard or no dashboard” to the minimum observability required for a responsible launch versus the enhanced insight that could follow safely.

DecisionLaunch with critical health and adoption metrics; deliver the richer dashboard as a fast follow within ~2 weeks.
Strategy

Tied launch scope to the accountant journey and measurable business outcomes.

Framework

Integrated plan, weekly steerco, decision log, readiness reviews, and reusable Go/No-Go criteria.

Dashboard

Signups, funnel drop-off, SLA, defects, adoption behavior, support signals, and revenue forecast.

Enablement

Clear owners, launch command center, escalation paths, and shared operating playbook.

Weekly decisions

Prioritization & scale

Grouped requests into capability programs, exposed capacity collisions, and sequenced work against the critical path.

Metrics & dashboard

Tracked owner coverage, alignment state, aging decisions, dependency hotspots, commitment confidence, and escalation volume.

Team enablement

Risk management

Separated unresolved decisions from delivery risks, assigned closure dates, and escalated only where leadership intervention changed the outcome.
